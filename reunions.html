Frequently asked questions

How many people can The Burland accommodate?

Up to 100+ guests for daytime events and gatherings, with room to sleep 40+ on site — one of the largest private event spaces in Eastern Idaho.

How far in advance should we book?

Summer weekends fill quickly, especially around Yellowstone season. Plan to reach out 6–12 months ahead for peak dates, and check the availability calendar for what's open.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Absolutely. The full kitchen is yours to use — bring family recipes, hire a caterer, or do a potluck. Catering isn't provided, but the kitchen is fully equipped for large-group cooking.

Is the venue accessible for elderly guests?

Yes. The Burland is designed to be comfortable for all ages. Reach out with specific accessibility questions and we'll do our best to accommodate your family.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on guest count, length of stay, and time of year. Contact us for a custom quote for your reunion.

Can we do a site visit before booking?

Yes, and we encourage it — there's nothing like seeing the space in person. Reach out through the contact page and we'll set up a time.

Ready to plan your family reunion?

Dates fill up fast, especially in summer. Check availability now or reach out and we'll help plan a reunion your family will talk about for years.
