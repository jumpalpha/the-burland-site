Area Attractions

Eastern Idaho is worth the trip

St. Anthony sits at the center of some of the most spectacular outdoor scenery in the American West.

~45 min away

Yellowstone National Park

Old Faithful, the Grand Prismatic Spring, bison, wolves, and geysers around every bend. The West Yellowstone entrance is closest, and the drive through Island Park is one of the most scenic routes in the region.

~40 min away

Mesa Falls

Upper Mesa Falls drops 114 feet over ancient volcanic rock. A short, accessible boardwalk leads the whole family to the viewing platform, and the Mesa Falls Scenic Byway is a scenic 28-mile drive on its own.

Minutes away

St. Anthony Sand Dunes

Miles of white sand dunes draw ATVers, sandboarders, hikers, and families — one of Idaho's most unexpected natural wonders, right in your own backyard.

World-class

Fishing on Henry's Fork

One of the most celebrated fly fishing rivers in North America. Island Park Reservoir, Ashton Reservoir, and Henry's Lake are all within easy reach.

All abilities

Hiking & outdoor adventures

The Caribou-Targhee National Forest surrounds the region with hundreds of miles of trails, including the Ashton-Tetonia rail-to-trail. The Teton Range is just an hour away.
