This is Episode 4 of "Burland Basecamp," a podcast for families planning a reunion at The Burland, a restored 1912 building in St. Anthony, Idaho. The episode introduces listeners to the volcanic landscape of Eastern Idaho -- lava fields, cinder cones, lava tube caves, and the atomic history built on top of it all. Most guests come for Yellowstone and the Tetons; this episode shows them what they would otherwise drive past. Cover the close-to-home spots (Sand Dunes, Menan Buttes) and build toward the full-day Craters of the Moon trip, with EBR-I on the way. Keep the tone curious and enthusiastic -- this is a genuine surprise for most visitors. Follow the source's ground rules exactly: no drive times or distances, no invented fees or hours.
