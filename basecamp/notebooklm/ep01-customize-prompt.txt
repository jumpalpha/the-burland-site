This is Episode 1 of "Burland Basecamp," a podcast for families planning a reunion at The Burland, a restored 1912 building in St. Anthony, Idaho. Welcome listeners to the show and give the big picture: what's nearby, and how to mix one or two big day trips (Yellowstone, the Tetons) with easy close-to-home days (the sand dunes, Mesa Falls, Warm River). Warm and practical, for grandparents and parents. Follow the source's ground rules exactly: no drive times or distances, no "45 minutes from Yellowstone," no invented room or history details. Aim for about 10 minutes.
