### Episode 5: Winter at The Burland

- **Safety.** Every time snowmobiling or off-trail skiing comes up, there should be a reminder to check avalanche and weather conditions, ride with others, and consider a guide.
- **Mesa Falls in winter.** It should say the road is plowed only partway and the falls are reached by snowmobile or skis in winter. Stop if it suggests driving to the falls in winter.
- **Sand Dunes.** The big dunes are closed from January 1 to April 1 for wintering elk, deer, and moose. Stop if it suggests a winter dune trip after New Year's.
- **Yellowstone in winter.** Travel inside the park is by guided snowmobile or snowcoach, booked ahead, mostly from West Yellowstone. Season dates should be "generally mid-December to mid-March, check first," not fixed dates.
- **Sleigh rides.** The National Elk Refuge rides in Jackson generally run mid to late December into early April. Stop on exact dates.
- **Hot springs.** Heise keeps its hot pools open all year, but its summer pool and waterslide are summer only. Green Canyon has more limited off-season hours.
- **Small numbers to listen for.** Upper Mesa Falls about 114 feet. Kelly Canyon about 26 runs. The Grizzly and Wolf Discovery Center is open every day of the year.
- **Holiday stays.** It should tell people to ask The Burland about holiday dates, not promise any.

### Episode 6: Rainy days and kids

- **Yellowstone Bear World (most important).** Bear World is closed for the 2026 season and reopens in April 2027. The episode should treat it as summer-only, for a rainy summer day. Stop if it tells people to go this winter or says it is open now.
- **Summer-only extras.** The Porter Park carousel and the Idaho Falls Zoo should also be described as summer-season, not winter plans.
- **Year-round list.** Open in winter: The Burland's game room and media room, the Museum of Idaho, the Grizzly and Wolf Discovery Center, and the Heise hot pools. Green Canyon is open in winter with more limited hours.
- **Teton Flood Museum.** The dam failed on June 5, 1976. It should tell people to check the museum's location and hours first, since they have changed and depend on volunteers.
- **Museum of Idaho.** Traveling exhibits plus the permanent Idaho exhibit, "Way Out West." Stop if it names a specific current exhibit.
