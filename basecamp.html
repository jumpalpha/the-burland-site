The Burland story

A 1912 building, rebuilt for families

The Burland was built in 1912 in downtown St. Anthony. Since 2020 it has been restored from the roof down, with new wood floors, new ceilings, and the original character kept wherever it could be saved.

Ready-made plans

Three easy reunion days

Mix and match these with days at The Burland for games, meals, and time together.

Big day out

Yellowstone in a day

  1. Early Leave with packed lunches, through Island Park.
  2. Next Enter at West Yellowstone.
  3. Mid-morning Grand Prismatic Spring before the parking lot fills.
  4. Midday Old Faithful, lunch, and the boardwalks.
  5. Evening Dinner in West Yellowstone, then home.
Close to home

Dunes, falls & a river

  1. Morning St. Anthony Sand Dunes while the sand is cool.
  2. Noon Picnic at Warm River.
  3. Afternoon Upper and Lower Mesa Falls.
  4. Evening Back to The Burland for dinner and the game room.
Little kids & grandparents

Easy day, rain or shine

  1. Morning Yellowstone Bear World drive-through.
  2. Lunch Rexburg, then the Porter Park carousel.
  3. Afternoon Teton Flood Museum if it rains, or Menan Buttes if it doesn't.
  4. Evening Green Canyon Hot Springs, then movie night.
