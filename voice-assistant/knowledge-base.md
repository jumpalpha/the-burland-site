## About
- The Burland is a restored historic building, built in 1912, in downtown St. Anthony, Idaho.
- Built for family reunions, weddings, and gatherings where everyone stays under one roof.
- "Not a hotel, not a conference center — a family home, made bigger."
- Over 10,000 square feet across three floors.
- About 45 minutes from Yellowstone (West Yellowstone entrance closest).
- 10–15 minutes from both Rexburg, Idaho temples.
- Easy access from Rexburg and Idaho Falls.
- Contact: call or text 208-709-1403, email burlandevents@gmail.com, website theburland.com.

## Spaces and amenities
- Multiple private family suites / living spaces; sleeps 40+ on site.
- Ballroom space for dinners, dancing, celebrations. Named spaces on the rental form: Aspen Ballroom, Game room, Bear Den, Loft, Whitetail Room, Movie/Play room, Buffalo Herd, Wolf Den, Moose Crossing, Fishing Pond. Renters can request the whole building.
- Dedicated gaming / activity space for kids and teens (ping-pong, air hockey, arcade corner shown in gallery).
- Full kitchen, equipped for large-group cooking. Renters cook, bring a caterer, or do a potluck. Catering is NOT provided.
- Weddings: ceremony and reception both on site; bridal suite and groom's lounge; tables and chairs; dance floor; sound system / PA; lighting options; photo backdrops; heating and cooling; Wi-Fi; restrooms; ADA accessible; flexible vendor policy; off-site parking available.
- Accessibility: designed for all ages; ADA accessible per the weddings page. Specific accessibility questions → owner follows up.

## How booking works
1. Check the availability calendar (theburland.com/availability).
2. Send an inquiry (contact form, call/text 208-709-1403, or through this assistant).
3. The owner personally confirms the date. Nothing is booked automatically.
4. The owner sends a rental-agreement details link, then the agreement via SignNow.
5. The date is reserved only after the agreement is signed and the payment due at signing is received.
- Summer weekends fill fast; reach out 6–12 months ahead for peak dates.
