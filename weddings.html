A wedding that feels like yours

Most venues feel like a convention hall with flowers. The Burland is different. With over 10,000 square feet across three floors, there's room for every generation — grandparents, kids, everyone in between — to be fully part of your day.

For couples planning a temple wedding, The Burland is just 10–15 minutes from both Rexburg, Idaho temples. The celebration can begin the moment your family arrives.
